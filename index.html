About EcoTrack

EcoTrack exists so buyers can see exactly how ethical and sustainable each supplier in a product's journey really is, instead of trusting a made-up number. Every supplier's Eco Score is built from a public checklist of real, verifiable practices tied to two United Nations Sustainable Development Goals — so anyone can see exactly why a supplier scored the way they did, and what they'd need to improve.

SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth (Target 8.8)

Focuses on protecting labor rights and promoting safe, secure working environments for all workers, including fair pay, no child or forced labor, and safe factory conditions.

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption & Production (Target 12.5)

Focuses on reducing waste through recycling, cutting pollution from production and transport, and moving supply chains toward cleaner materials and logistics.

How the Eco Score is Calculated

Every supplier is scored out of 100 points using a 20-point checklist. Each item a supplier can verifiably tick off is worth 5 points. Scores are edited from the Suppliers tab so they always stay up to date and auditable.
